**The Bell Beneath the Roots**

At dusk, Mara Venn knocks on your door holding a basket covered with a damp cloth.

“My brother went to mend the bee fences this morning. His basket came back.”

Beneath the cloth lie three sprigs of rosemary, white with frost. The evening is warm. A loop of red thread binds their stems in a knot you recognize: the village custom for remembering someone who has died.

Mara’s brother, **Oren**, was alive at breakfast.

You stand with Mara at the woodland stile. Beyond it, his bootprints follow the old charcoal path. Beside them runs a narrow groove, as though something heavy was dragged **toward the village**. The basket was found here.

Deep among the trees, a small bell rings twice.

Mara catches her breath. “That’s his bee bell.”

A third note sounds—from beneath the roots of the ash tree beside you.

You could examine the frozen herbs and knot, ask Mara about Oren’s errand, investigate the roots, follow the tracks, or try speaking with the ash. There is still enough quiet here to prepare a talisman or Commune.
