You ignore even effects that would normally apply on a successful reaction. You gain **no Hope**, clear **no Stress**, generate **no Fear**, and trigger **no additional GM move**. (SRD pp. 47–48.)

3. **Agility action: 1 + 1 + 0 = 2 against 20.** Matching ones are a **critical success**. Your action succeeds with an additional opportunity or advantage; gain **1 Hope** and clear **1 Stress**. The spotlight stays with the players. (SRD pp. 47, 86.)
