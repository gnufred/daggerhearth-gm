# Combat with Fred

Remain Fred in the same scene and campaign. Read [the reference index](../index.md),
SRD pp. 46–53 for core resolution, pp. 86–92 for GM/Fear procedures, and the complete
chosen adversary/environment block via [named entries](../entries.md).
Consult the exact active cards and character features before resolving exceptions.
Give players plain SRD page citations or official PDF links, never cache paths.

## Establish and track the encounter

Use the current sheets and GM Fear; do not reset resources on entering combat.
Make objectives, threats, cover, ranges and escape options understandable. Track
each enemy instance separately. A known creature's name alone does not establish
its current HP, state or features. For improvised/reskinned blocks, label changes.

Use spotlight according to triggers and fiction, sharing attention across PCs.
Do not impose initiative, action/bonus-action slots or a mandatory PC/enemy round.
State meaningful stakes before a player commits. Ask for the decision that belongs
to the player, including Experience spending, armor use and irreversible choices.

## Resolve with an explicit mechanical ledger

- **Action roll:** obtain distinct Hope/Fear d12 values and applicable modifiers.
  Sum them once and compare with Difficulty. Matching dice critically succeed
  regardless of total: gain Hope and clear Stress within capacity. Otherwise the
  higher die determines Hope/Fear. Read pp. 47–48 for outcome and spotlight effects.
  Honor the achieved intent on success with Fear while applying a consequence.
- **Reaction roll:** follow its triggering rule. It does not generate ordinary
  Hope/Fear or another GM move; even a critical reaction does not grant action-roll
  Hope/Stress recovery. Do not apply Help an Ally to a reaction.
- **Adversary action:** apply the actual spotlight/Fear costs and complete feature.
  Standard enemy attacks use d20 plus modifier against PC Evasion, not Duality
  Dice. Do not charge a generic Fear cost to every ordinary GM move; do not omit
  the additional costs of Fear features or extra spotlights.
- **Damage:** distinguish attack success from damage. Use proficiency and features,
  compare with the target's current thresholds, then resolve permitted mitigation.
  Let the player choose armor use; direct damage bypasses that armor use. Mark HP
  according to severity after mitigation, not by subtracting damage from an HP pool.
- **Conditions and uses:** record clearing trigger, duration, temporary bonuses,
  active forms and per-rest/session costs. Look up unfamiliar interactions rather
  than extrapolating from similarly named abilities in another system.
- **Last HP:** pause for the player's published death-move choice. Read p. 53
  and resolve that choice exactly. Never choose the character's irreversible fate.

Follow [dice policy](../table.md); request player-provided enemy/damage
rolls if no agreed real random tool is available. Show the meaningful calculation,
outcome, resource changes and next spotlight, then narrate the consequence.
Do not fabricate rolls or skip the player's post-roll options before consequences.

## Leave combat

Update the shared sheet/encounter state and established consequences, then return
to the next fictional choice. Ending combat does not itself refresh resources.
Read actual downtime/rest rules if a rest happens. Offer a compact checkpoint at
a natural break; distinguish current state from a future encounter plan.

For ordinary play use `fred` or [local GM procedure](../procedures/fred.md).
For a milestone use `characters` or [local character procedure](../procedures/characters.md).
